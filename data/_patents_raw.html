  • Brevet 2: e-Ochestra (Conducting Made Easier)



    Our Team EzzInova propose a new and an innovate solution ‘e-Ochestra’, where young musicians and amateurs can learn the basic conducting moves in a controllable and flexible environment using sensor as orchestra baton to communicate and hold a virtual orchestra theater.
    ‘e-Ochestra’ a platform where young musicians and amateurs can learn the conducting moves in a controllable, interactive and flexible environment. The ‘e-Ochestra’ hold automatically a predefined fermata note, as long as the conductor user tells them the tempo, like as ups and downs of a piece. The learner uses a specific orchestra baton with integrated wireless sensor for gesture detection. We developed an optimal algorithm of gesture recognition based on hash code, and machine learning technique for detecting automatically the following signal with the used speed of gesture to control a group of fermata notes. The baton consists of a vibrating alarm controlled by the system to enquire the learner on handling errors for a new try.
    Before using the ‘e-Ochestra’ an expert or amateur can add the instruments virtual orchestra theater on the specific places and select with Support Agent. The Support Agent check the right choice of instruments places in orchestra theater using the same idea of last amazing solution of Microsoft “HOLOLENS”.

    déficience auditive.



Domaine de l’invention
La présente invention a trait à un dispositif et un procédé d’aide à la conduite des véhicules
pour des individus ayant une déficience auditive et plus particulièrement la mise en oeuvre d’un
dispositif d’affichage d’informations utiles au profit d’un conducteur déficient auditif
moyennant un procédé adéquat.
